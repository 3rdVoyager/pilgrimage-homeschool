<div class="page-section">
  <h2>Membership Requirements</h2>
  <p>Team members must be:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>8th - 12th graders (by age) to compete on the HS team (Div C)</li>
    <li>5th - 9th graders (by age) to compete on the MS team (Div B)</li>
    <li>3rd - 6th graders (by age) to compete on the ES team (Div A) for invitationals and tournaments</li>
  </ul>
  <p>K-2nd siblings (of ES/MS/HS teammates) can participate by parents having access to our online materials for parents to use with students in their home. There is no fee for K-2nd siblings to participate in the team as they are not permitted to compete in tournaments. But they are welcome to make use of our resources as they learn about Science Olympiad along with their older sibling. We will not have K-2nd graders on the team that do not have older siblings on the competition teams.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Registered as homeschoolers</li>
    <li>Located in Montgomery or Howard County, Maryland</li>
  </ul>
</div>
